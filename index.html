Our Story

Crafted with Passion,
Served with Grace

✦

Born from a deep love for the art of baking, Wae Cicu Bake began as a humble dream in the heart of Jember. We believe that a cake is not merely dessert — it is an emotion, a celebration, a memory carefully folded into every layer.

Every morning, our artisans arrive before the city wakes, selecting only the finest locally-sourced ingredients. Our recipes carry the wisdom of tradition and the excitement of modern patisserie — resulting in flavors that linger long after the last bite.
